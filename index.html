Executive Summary

What changed, why, and what to review

This report documents the v2 copy rollout across all 21 Redstone landing pages, completed in response to 18 copy-comment directives from Eric. Every change is text-only — no structural deletions, no layout modifications, no hero images touched. All edits were audit-verified against each directive with a pass/fail scan across the full page set.

The primary focus of this round was precision and accuracy. Key changes include: removing the 48-hour quote claim that could not be guaranteed operationally; standardizing on “ISO 9001 certified” instead of the shorthand “ISO-certified”; replacing “program” with “parts” in all CTAs per Eric’s framing preference; adding Monterrey factory specificity and USMCA/6-8 week lead-time language across the 9 Mexico-targeted pages; and removing AWS-welder language from the 7 sheet-metal pages where it appeared.

The tolerance update on CNC pages (±0.0005” → ±0.001”) and the removal of 5-axis machining references across all 21 pages were applied uniformly. Ohio references have been removed with no Seattle or alternative city substituted, as directed. The Qualifications section was explicitly preserved on all pages despite being flagged for review — Eric confirmed keep.

A small set of claims are flagged in Section 6 for Eric’s sign-off before this copy goes live: the “2,000+ programs” stat, the IATF 16949 references remaining in trust blocks, and the client logo set currently on pages but not appearing on Redstone’s own website.
